Why galactic discs
are not flat

I am an astrophysicist at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I work on warps, bending waves and the vertical structure of galactic discs, using N-body and hydrodynamical simulations alongside data from Gaia, APOGEE, GALAH and LAMOST.

Research

The Milky Way's disc is warped at its edges and carries waves throughout. Those distortions are not noise around a flat equilibrium. They are a record of how the Galaxy was built and what surrounds it. My work is about reading that record: which mechanism produces which signature, and what each one tells you.

Warps and the thick disc

Stars that form inside a warped gas disc do not stay at the warp. They are carried to large heights and pick up the kinematics we normally use to identify thick-disc membership.

Bending waves from gas accretion

Gas arriving along a direction misaligned with the disc, and arriving irregularly rather than smoothly, excites bending waves that travel through it. This gives a driver that works continuously, so a galaxy does not need a recent merger to have a disturbed disc.

Breathing waves and spirals

Breathing motions compress and expand the disc rather than displacing it. Measuring their pattern speeds showed they match the spiral structure, so they are driven from inside the disc. Bending waves trace an outside influence; breathing waves trace an inside one. That division turns a disturbed disc into a diagnostic.

Warp precession and the halo

If the Galactic warp precesses, the rate at which it does would constrain the shape of the dark matter halo driving it. Measuring that rate is the hard part, and the answers so far are contested. A good deal of my current work is on the method itself: what a precession measurement is actually sensitive to, and how far a given one can be trusted.

Code

POGONA

A Python library for analysing N-body and N-body+SPH simulations. It handles large simulation outputs and extracts the kinematic and structural diagnostics that each group would otherwise rebuild for itself. It is used by research groups beyond my own.

Repository Documentation

I also maintain pipelines that cross-match Gaia astrometry against the APOGEE, GALAH and LAMOST spectroscopic surveys, and methods for pulling weak dynamical signals out of noisy, selection-limited samples.
